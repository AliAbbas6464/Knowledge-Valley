Live evening classes for finance professionals working in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and across the Gulf. This page puts everything that is specific to Saudi Arabia and the UAE in one place: your class time, your costs in riyals and dirhams, your exam options and the local guides.

Quick answer

Knowledge Valley teaches the US CMA (Certified Management Accountant) live online, in the evening, for finance professionals in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and the UAE. Classes run 8 to 10 pm Saudi time (9 to 11 pm in the UAE), three evenings a week. Each part costs SAR 2,500 (about AED 2,450), paid in three monthly installments. IMA exam fees are paid separately to IMA in US dollars.

  • Part 1 and Part 2 each take five months: 16 weeks of 48 live classes, then a full review month with 12 mock exams.
  • IMA offers three exam windows a year: January to February, May to June and September to October.
  • Looking for the regulator? The Capital Market Authority is a different “CMA”. This page is about the US certification from IMA.

From the Gulf to your CMA certificate

Six steps, in order, with the Gulf details that matter at each one.

  1. Join IMA and enrolBecome an IMA member and pay the CMA entrance fee. IMA bills in US dollars, so your bank converts the amount.
  2. Pick your testing windowChoose January–February, May–June or September–October, and count back to your start date.
  3. Join live evening classes48 live classes per part, case-based question practice, topic tests and 12 mock exams.
  4. Register for the exam with IMABuy your exam registration. This does not reserve a seat.
  5. Book your seat with PrometricChoose a centre and a date. Rescheduling inside the deadline costs a fee, so book with care.
  6. Sit the exam, then get certifiedPass both parts within three years, then apply to IMA for your CMA certificate.

Questions from the Gulf

Straight answers for candidates in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

What time are the classes in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Classes run from 8 to 10 pm Saudi time, which is 9 to 11 pm in the UAE and Oman. The same time applies in Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain as in Saudi Arabia. Part 1 meets on Wednesday, Friday and Sunday. Part 2 meets on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

Which CMA course has evening classes in Saudi time?

Knowledge Valley teaches live CMA (US) classes in the evening, 8 to 10 pm Saudi time (9 to 11 pm in the UAE), three evenings a week. Part 1 runs on Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, and Part 2 on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, so you can study after your working day. See the Part 1 and Part 2 class plans.

Can I pay for a CMA course in installments in Saudi riyals?

Yes. Each part costs SAR 2,500, paid in three equal monthly installments of about SAR 833. You pay for Part 1 first, and for Part 2 only when you start it. IMA’s own fees are separate and are paid to IMA in US dollars. See CMA exam fees in USD, AED and SAR.

Are there CMA classes in Urdu or Hindi for students in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Classes are taught mainly in English. Your mentor explains difficult points in Urdu or Hindi where it helps, and uses Arabic occasionally. Ask about this in your free orientation if the language matters to you.

How much does a CMA course cost in Saudi Arabia and Dubai?

Course fees differ between institutes, so compare what each one includes. At Knowledge Valley each part costs SAR 2,500 (about AED 2,450), paid in three monthly installments, for students in Saudi Arabia and the UAE alike. IMA’s membership, entrance and exam fees are extra: about $1,685 in the first year for a professional member (about SAR 6,319 or AED 6,188). See the fees section above.

Can I join from Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain or Oman?

Yes. The classes are live online, so you can join from any country in the Gulf. Check the time in the table above. Message us on WhatsApp if you want to confirm the batch for your country.

Where do I take the CMA exam if I live in the Gulf?

CMA exams are computer-based and are administered through Prometric. IMA Middle East lists exam locations in Dubai and Abu Dhabi for the UAE. For Saudi Arabia, use the Prometric locator to see centres near you. IMA has also offered remote proctoring through Prometric, so check IMA’s current rules.

Is the CMA recognised in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes, as an international professional certification. In Saudi Arabia, HRDF accredited it, according to IMA. In the UAE, the Ministry of Finance signed an MoU with IMA in August 2025. It is a certification, not a licence, and each employer decides how much weight to give it.

How much does the CMA cost in SAR and AED?

Coaching with Knowledge Valley is SAR 2,500 per part (about AED 2,450), paid in three monthly installments. IMA fees are about SAR 6,319 or AED 6,188 in the first year for a professional member. For both parts at the first attempt, the total is about SAR 11,319 or AED 11,085.

Can my employer or HRDF pay for it?

HRDF funding is for Saudi nationals only and has conditions: the CMA must be on its approved list, and the employer must not have paid for the certificate. Expatriates should ask their employer about a training budget. Read HRDF’s page for the current rules before you pay.

How do I choose a CMA course in Saudi Arabia or the UAE?

Check five things: whether classes are live or recorded, what time they run, how clear the fee is, whether you can pay in installments, and how much exam practice is included (case-based questions and full mock exams). Because most candidates work full time, evening classes matter. Our guide on live and recorded CMA classes explains the difference.

How much do CMA courses cost in Saudi Arabia and Dubai?

Fees vary by provider and format, and IMA fees are separate from coaching fees. At Knowledge Valley, each part is SAR 2,500 (about AED 2,450) in three monthly installments. IMA fees come on top, about SAR 6,319 or AED 6,188 in the first year for a professional member.

Is “CMA” in Saudi Arabia or the UAE the US CMA or the Capital Market Authority?

Both use the same letters. The US CMA is the Certified Management Accountant certification from IMA for finance professionals. The Capital Market Authority is a financial regulator. Add “US”, “IMA” or “Certified Management Accountant” to your search to find the certification.

How many hours a week do I need?

Most working students study about 8 to 10 hours a week, including the live classes. Each part takes five months with us: 16 weeks of classes and a full review month.
