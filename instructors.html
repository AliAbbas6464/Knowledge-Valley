FAQs

Questions about choosing your CMA (US) teacher

What should I look for in a CMA (US) teacher?

Look for four things: the teacher holds the CMA (US) himself, has real industry experience, has taught for years, and teaches live so you can ask questions. Ali Abbas, who leads Knowledge Valley, has all four: he is CMA (US) qualified, has 18+ years in finance including CFO and Internal Audit Manager roles, has taught for 10+ years, and teaches live and interactive classes.

How do I know if a CMA teacher is good before I join?

Check his qualification and experience, watch a sample lesson, and attend a free class. You can watch Ali's lessons on this page and book a free orientation to meet him before you pay anything.

Is a CMA teacher with real industry experience better?

For most students, yes. A teacher who has worked as a CFO and an Internal Audit Manager can show how a concept is used in real companies, which makes topics like budgeting, costing, controls and risk easier to understand and remember.

Is there a CMA teacher in Saudi Arabia and the UAE who knows the Gulf market?

Yes. Ali worked for 7+ years in Dubai with a company headquartered in the UAE and operating across the whole Gulf, so he knows the Saudi and UAE market first-hand. He teaches live evening classes, 8 to 10 pm Saudi time.

Can I take CMA live classes with a real teacher, not recordings?

Yes. Knowledge Valley classes are live and interactive, taught by Ali. We do not use pre-recorded lectures, and you can ask questions during the class.

Is there a CMA teacher who explains in English with Urdu or Hindi?

Yes. Classes are mainly in English. Ali uses Urdu or Hindi words where they make a concept easier to grasp, and Arabic occasionally.

Can I try a CMA class before I join?

Yes. Book a free orientation and meet Ali in a free class first. You decide after you have seen how he teaches.

Can I ask my CMA teacher questions and get personal help?

Yes. You can ask questions in the live class, and you get personal mentor support between classes to clear doubts and review weak areas until exam day.
