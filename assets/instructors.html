CEO Knowledge Valley

Ali Abbas is the CEO and Mentor of Knowledge Valley and the lead trainer behind its CMA (US) coaching program. A Certified Management Accountant working as a Sr. Manager in Finance and Auditing, he brings over 10 years of teaching experience to every batch — guiding working professionals and finance graduates across Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and the wider Gulf region through both parts of the CMA exam.

His practical experience spans Finance, Internal Audit, Internal Controls, ERP Systems and Financial Management within Gulf corporate environments — which is why every CMA topic is explained through real business examples instead of pure textbook theory. He teaches every class live, never pre-recorded, so students can ask questions and get immediate clarification. Beyond live sessions, he personally follows up with students to clear doubts, review weak areas, and keep every candidate on track toward exam day — a relationship that continues for as long as the CMA journey takes.
