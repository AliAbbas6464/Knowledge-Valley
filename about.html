Our story

Why Ali Abbas started Knowledge Valley

A CMA who has worked in senior finance roles and in the classroom, building the coaching he believed Gulf professionals needed.

Ali Abbas is CMA (US) qualified and has worked in finance for more than 18 years, including CFO and Manager Internal Audit roles and 7+ years in Dubai with a company operating across the Gulf. All through his career he kept teaching, and he has now taught CMA students for more than 10 years.

Since 2018 he has taught CMA online, and Knowledge Valley grew from that work into an institute built for working professionals: live classes in the evening, a clear plan to follow, and real access to the teacher. Anyone can see how the classes work before joining, through a free orientation and a free trial class.

The format keeps improving with student feedback. Each part now has four months of live coaching, followed by one month of testing and mock exams, so you are fully prepared for the exam. Today Knowledge Valley has coached hundreds of CMA students, and its WhatsApp community has grown past 1,000 members.

FAQs

Questions about Knowledge Valley and CMA coaching

What is Knowledge Valley?

Knowledge Valley (Knowledge Valley Online Coaching Center) is an online CMA (US) coaching institute for working professionals in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and across the Gulf. It has taught CMA online since 2018, in live evening classes on Saudi time, led by Ali Abbas, CMA (US).

Is there a CMA institute with live evening classes for working professionals in the Gulf?

Yes. Knowledge Valley classes are live, held three days a week from 8 to 10 pm Saudi time, so they fit around a normal working day. Part 1 runs on Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, and Part 2 on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.

Can I pay for CMA coaching in installments, and in SAR?

Yes. CMA coaching costs SAR 2,500 per part (about AED 2,450 or USD 667), paid in 3 equal, flexible monthly installments of about SAR 833. AED and USD amounts are approximate.

Is there a CMA institute that explains in Urdu or Hindi?

Yes. Classes are mainly in English, with Urdu or Hindi words used where they make a concept easier to understand, and Arabic occasionally.

Can I take CMA one part at a time?

Yes. You can take Part 1 or Part 2 on its own, with no obligation to take the other. Each part has its own 48 classes and its own 3 installments, paid when you start that part.

How do I choose a CMA coaching institute?

Check six things: whether classes are live and not recorded, whether the timings suit your job, whether the fees and payment terms are clear, whether you are taught in a language you follow, how much practice and how many mock exams you get, and whether you can try a class before paying.

Is there a CMA community for students in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes. Knowledge Valley runs a WhatsApp community of 1,000+ members, including ex-students, CMA candidates and qualified CMAs. It is open to every CMA aspirant, not only our students.

Is Knowledge Valley a recorded course or live classes?

Live classes. All classes are live and interactive, and we do not use pre-recorded lectures. Personal mentor support is available alongside the classes.

Is there a free trial class before I pay?

Yes. You can book a free orientation and try a free class first. You pay only if you are satisfied, and then in 3 equal monthly installments.

Does Knowledge Valley teach only CMA (US)?

Yes. Knowledge Valley is a CMA (US) specialist. We also provide corporate training for companies that want to train their finance teams.
