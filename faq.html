The CMA and the exam10 questions

What the CMA is, who can take it and how the exam works.

What is the CMA (US) certification, and what does CMA stand for?

CMA stands for Certified Management Accountant. It is a management accounting and financial management certification from IMA, the Institute of Management Accountants, in the United States. You pass two exam parts: Part 1 covers Financial Planning, Performance and Analytics, and Part 2 covers Strategic Financial Management.

CMA Part 1 →CMA Part 2 →

What are the eligibility requirements?

You need IMA membership, the CMA entrance fee, a bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or a related professional certification, and two years of professional experience in management accounting or financial management. The experience can be completed before you start or within seven years of passing. Always confirm the latest rules on IMA’s website.

How is the exam structured?

Each part is a 4-hour exam: 100 multiple-choice questions (75% of the score) and 2 case-based questions (25%). You need a scaled score of 360 out of 500 to pass. You can take the two parts in either order.

Exam format on the Part 1 page →

What is the passing score for the CMA exam?

You need a scaled score of at least 360 out of 500 on each part. In the multiple-choice section you also need at least 50% to move on to the case-based questions.

Exam format on the Part 1 page →

Is the CMA exam hard?

The CMA is a demanding exam, but most of the difficulty comes from not practising enough questions, not from the concepts. That is why every topic in our classes ends with questions and tests, and why the review month is all mock exams.

How we prepare you →

How long does it take to become a CMA?

With Knowledge Valley each part takes five months, so both parts take about ten months if you study them one after the other. IMA requires you to pass both parts within three years of paying the entrance fee. You also need two years of qualifying experience.

Plan your exam year →

Should I take Part 1 or Part 2 first?

IMA does not require a fixed order. Beginners should normally start with Part 1, because it builds the base for Part 2. If you are already part-qualified (ACCA, ACA, CPA or similar), you can start with either part.

When can I sit the exam?

IMA offers three testing windows a year: January to February, May to June and September to October. You register with IMA, then book your seat with Prometric.

Do I need to join IMA before I start classes?

No. You can start classes before you register with IMA. Join IMA and pay the entrance fee when you are ready to plan your exam date.

What if I do not pass on my first attempt?

We review your official score report with you, find the weak areas and rebuild your study plan around them, so a retake is targeted and not a full restart. A retake costs the IMA exam fee again.

Is the US CMA recognised in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes, as an international professional certification. In Saudi Arabia, HRDF accredited the CMA, according to IMA. In the UAE, the Ministry of Finance signed an MoU with IMA in August 2025. It is a certification, not a licence, and each employer decides how much weight to give it.

Is the US CMA worth it in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

It can be, depending on your role and goals. Saudi and UAE employers list the CMA in finance job posts, usually as preferred, and it builds planning, budgeting, costing and decision-making skills. It is a certification, not a licence, so check the job posts for the roles you want.

Which is better in the Gulf: CMA, ACCA or CPA?

They serve different paths. The CMA focuses on management accounting and financial management. ACCA and CPA are broader accounting and audit qualifications, and in Saudi Arabia the SOCPA route is the one for the Saudi CPA. Many professionals hold more than one.

Is “CMA” the US certification or the Capital Market Authority?

Both use the same letters. The US CMA is the Certified Management Accountant certification from IMA. The Capital Market Authority is a financial regulator. Add “US” or “IMA” to your search to find the certification.
