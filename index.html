Part 2 Overview

CMA USA Part 2 — Strategic Financial Management

CMA USA Part 2 is the second of the two exams that make up the US CMA certification, focused on Strategic Financial Management — how organizations analyze their own financial statements, raise and manage capital, make decisions using cost and pricing data, manage risk, decide where to invest, and hold themselves to a professional code of ethics.

Where Part 1 looks inward at planning, budgeting and cost control, Part 2 looks at the bigger financial picture: reading a company's financial position, weighing financing and investment choices, and applying analytical judgment to real business decisions. In Detail

CMA USA Part 2 Subjects

What each topic actually covers, beyond the syllabus title.

📊

Financial Statement Analysis 20%

Reading and interpreting financial statements using ratio analysis, and assessing a company's profitability, liquidity and solvency. Also introduces the basics of market and business valuation — skills used constantly in real financial analysis roles.

🏦

Corporate Finance 20%

How companies manage risk and return, working capital, and raise capital through debt or equity, including corporate restructuring and the basics of international finance. Directly relevant to anyone in corporate treasury or finance functions.

🧮

Business Decision Analysis 25%

The largest section — cost-volume-profit and marginal analysis, pricing decisions, relevant costing, and using business analytics to support decisions. Tests applied business decision analysis: turning numbers into a recommendation, not just a calculation.

🛡️

Enterprise Risk Management 10%

Identifying different types of enterprise risk, and the strategies organizations use to mitigate them, including a basic introduction to derivatives. A smaller section, but one that ties directly into governance and internal-controls thinking from Part 1.

💹

Capital Investment Decisions 10%

The capital budgeting process for capital investment decisions — discounted cash flow methods, payback analysis, and evaluating the risk of a proposed investment before committing company funds to it.

⚖️

Professional Ethics 15%

The IMA Statement of Ethical Professional Practice and how it applies to real organizational scenarios. A meaningful 15% of the exam, tested through case-based ethical dilemmas rather than pure recall.

Exam Format

CMA USA Part 2 Exam Format

Like Part 1, CMA Part 2 is a four-hour exam made up of two sections.

  • 100 multiple-choice questions covering all six topic areas above. A minimum score of 50% on this section is required before the application section is scored.
  • An application section that follows the multiple-choice questions.
  • Starting with the September/October 2026 testing window, English-language CMA exams use case-based questions (CBQs) in this application section instead of the essay format used previously. Each CBQ presents a short business scenario (around 250 words) and asks a set of structured questions — such as calculations, drag-and-drop, fill-in-the-blank and select-from-a-list — rather than requiring a written essay response.
  • The six topic areas, total exam length and overall difficulty of Part 2 are unchanged — only the way the application section is answered has moved from essay writing to case-based questions.
Is This For You

Who Should Take CMA USA Part 2?

Part 2 is particularly relevant to:

  • Accounting and finance professionals
  • Management accounting professionals
  • Financial analysts
  • Finance managers
  • Business professionals involved in financial decision-making
  • Candidates who have completed, or are currently preparing for, CMA Part 1

You are not required to take Part 1 before Part 2 — the IMA allows either part to be attempted first. Many candidates do choose to start with Part 1, but Part 2 can be your starting point if it fits your background or study plan better.

Study Approach

How to Prepare for CMA USA Part 2

A practical approach that accounts for Part 2's heavier calculation and application load.

Understand the Concepts

Build a solid conceptual base for each topic before moving into formulas and calculations — this matters even more in a section-heavy subject like Business Decision Analysis.

2

Solve MCQs

Use your test results to identify specific weak spots and revise those directly, rather than re-reading the whole syllabus.

Practice Case-Based / Application Questions

Work through case-based scenario questions so you're comfortable applying concepts to a business situation, not just answering isolated MCQs.

6

Mock Exams & Time Management

Take full-length, timed mock exams to build stamina and get your pacing right across both the MCQ and application sections before exam day.

A Fair Question

Is CMA USA Part 2 Difficult?

Many candidates find Part 2 more calculation-intensive than Part 1, largely because Business Decision Analysis alone makes up a quarter of the exam and draws heavily on applied finance and cost concepts. Corporate Finance and Financial Statement Analysis add further quantitative weight, so comfort with numbers matters more here than in Part 1.

That said, difficulty still comes down to background, time available, and how consistently you practice rather than just review. Candidates from a finance or corporate-finance-heavy background often find Part 2 more intuitive, while others need extra time on the calculation-based sections.

Structured preparation makes the biggest difference — working through topic-wise questions, testing regularly, practicing case-based application scenarios, and sitting full mock exams under timed conditions. There's no shortcut and no guaranteed outcome, but a clear plan and consistent practice go a long way toward exam readiness.
