About Knowledge Valley

An online CMA (US) coaching institute for Gulf professionals

Knowledge Valley is an online CMA coaching institute for finance professionals in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider GCC. We prepare you for both parts of the CMA (US) exam: Part 1 (Financial Planning, Performance, and Analytics) and Part 2 (Strategic Financial Management).

Every class is live and interactive, taught by your mentor in the evening after office hours, with personal support whenever you need it, so you can keep working while you study. You get short notes, topic-wise and section-wise tests, case-based question practice and full mock exams. Quick answers

CMA (US) coaching in Saudi Arabia and the UAE: your questions answered

What is the best way to study CMA (US) online while working in Saudi Arabia or the UAE?

Live evening classes suit most working professionals. At Knowledge Valley, classes run 8–10 pm Saudi time on 3 days a week, with live interaction, short notes and regular tests. Most working students study around 8–10 hours a week, and a makeup class is arranged if a genuine problem makes you miss a session.

Are your CMA classes live or recorded?

All classes are taught live and interactively by your mentor, with personal support whenever you need it. We do not use pre-recorded lectures.

What are the CMA class timings for Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Classes are held in the evening, 8–10 pm Saudi time, for 1.5–2 hours per session on 3 days a week. That means you can attend after office hours from Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other Gulf cities.

Can I attend a free CMA demo class before paying?

Yes. You get a free one-to-one orientation and 1–2 free trial classes before you pay anything. You join only if you are satisfied with how we teach.

Can I pay the CMA course fee in installments?

Yes. The fee is SAR 2,500 per part (about AED 2,450 or USD 667), paid in 3 equal flexible monthly installments for each part. You pay Part 1 first, and Part 2 when you start it. IMA membership, entrance and exam fees are paid separately to IMA.

Do you teach CMA in Urdu or Hindi?

Classes are taught mainly in English, the language of the CMA exam. Where it helps you understand, your mentor explains difficult points in Urdu or Hindi, and occasionally in Arabic.

I am part-qualified in ACCA, CA or ICMAP. Can I start CMA (US)?

Yes. CMA is a separate US credential with only two parts, and you can start from where you are. In the free orientation we look at your progress and plan Part 1 and Part 2 around it.

How do you prepare students for the CMA case-based questions (CBQ)?

IMA has replaced the essay section of the exam with case-based questions. Our testing system includes case-based question practice, along with topic-wise tests, section-wise tests and full mock exams, so you practise the way the exam now asks.

Do you offer one-to-one CMA coaching and mentoring?

Yes. Every class is live and interactive, and you can discuss anything with your mentor one-to-one, and your mentor is available between classes. The free orientation is the best way to meet your mentor.

Do you provide CMA training for companies in Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes. We offer CMA (US) coaching and customised corporate training programs for finance teams, with workshops, assessments and institute-issued certificates. Message us on WhatsApp with your team’s needs.

Is CMA (US) worth it for accountants working in the Gulf?

For many finance professionals, CMA (US) is a respected credential in management accounting, planning, performance and controls. Whether it is right for you depends on your role, goals and employer. Our guides to the CMA salary in Saudi Arabia and the CMA salary in the UAE show typical pay ranges, and the free orientation helps you decide.
