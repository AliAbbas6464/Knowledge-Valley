Is it right for you?

Should you take CMA Part 1 or Part 2 first?

IMA does not set an order, so the choice is yours. Here is how we usually advise.

New to CMA

Start with Part 1. It builds the base in reporting, planning, costing and controls that Part 2 builds on.

Part-qualified (ACCA, ACA, CPA and similar)

You can start with either part. We will help you choose in your free orientation.

Graduate or finance professional

Commerce, finance and accounting backgrounds usually begin with Part 1.

Eligibility for the exam is set by IMA. Please confirm your own eligibility with IMA before you register.

Is CMA Part 1 difficult?

Part 1 covers a lot of ground in a short time, with many calculations and now case-based questions. Most of the difficulty comes from not practising enough questions, not from the concepts. That is why every topic in our plan ends with questions and tests, and why Month 5 is all mock exams.

FAQs

CMA Part 1 questions, answered

How long does CMA Part 1 take?

CMA Part 1 takes 5 months with us: 16 weeks (about 4 months) of 48 live classes, then a full review month with 12 mock exams. Plan on about 8 to 10 hours of study each week, including the classes.

How many classes are in CMA Part 1?

There are 48 live classes, held on Wednesday, Friday and Sunday from 8 to 10 pm Saudi time. Each class runs 1.5 to 2 hours. Topic-wise and section-wise tests are included within the 16 weeks.

How much does CMA Part 1 cost and can I pay in installments?

CMA Part 1 costs SAR 2,500 (about AED 2,450 or USD 667). You can pay in 3 equal, flexible monthly installments of about SAR 833. AED and USD amounts are approximate and depend on the exchange rate.

What is included in the CMA Part 1 fee?

The fee includes all 48 live classes, 2,000+ MCQs, 30 case-based questions (CBQs), 12 full mock exams, short notes and all topic-wise and section-wise tests. IMA membership, entrance and exam fees are paid separately to IMA.

What topics and weightage does CMA Part 1 cover?

Part 1 has six sections: A. External Financial Reporting Decisions (15%), B. Planning, Budgeting, and Forecasting (20%), C. Performance Management (20%), D. Cost Management (15%), E. Internal Controls (15%) and F. Technology and Analytics (15%). Our class-by-class plan lists every topic.

What is the CMA Part 1 exam format?

The CMA Part 1 exam is 4 hours long. It has 100 multiple-choice questions in 3 hours (75% of the score), then 2 case-based questions (CBQs) in 1 hour (25%). You need at least 50% on the multiple-choice questions to move on to the CBQs, and a scaled score of 360 out of 500 to pass.

Can I study CMA Part 1 while working full time?

Yes. Classes are live in the evening, 8 to 10 pm Saudi time, three days a week, so they fit around a normal working day. Most working professionals in our batches study about 8 to 10 hours a week.

Do I have to take Part 2 with you as well?

No. You can take Part 1 on its own with no obligation for Part 2. If you decide to continue, Part 2 has its own 48 classes and its own 3 installments, paid only when you start it.

Should I take CMA Part 1 or Part 2 first?

IMA does not require a fixed order. Beginners should normally start with Part 1, because it builds the base for Part 2. If you are already part-qualified (ACCA, ACA, CPA and similar), you can start with either part.

Are the classes in English?

Classes are mainly in English. We use Urdu or Hindi words where they make a concept easier to grasp, and Arabic occasionally.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Classes are live and interactive, and you can ask questions in the session. We do not use pre-recorded lectures. Personal mentor support is available alongside the classes.

How do I pass CMA Part 1?

Learn each topic in class, then practise questions on it the same week. Take the topic-wise and section-wise tests, study the mistakes, revise, and finish with full mock exams under exam conditions. The mix of MCQs and CBQs in the mocks matches the real exam.
