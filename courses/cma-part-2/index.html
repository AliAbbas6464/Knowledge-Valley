The exam

What is CMA Part 2?

CMA Part 2: Strategic Financial Management is the second of the two CMA (US) exams. It tests six sections, from financial statement analysis and corporate finance to business decisions, risk, capital investment and ethics.

Is CMA Part 2 difficult?

Part 2 is more numerical than Part 1. Ratio analysis, cost of capital, break-even, decision analysis and NPV all need regular practice, and Section C is the biggest at 25% of the exam. Most of the difficulty comes from not practising enough questions, not from the concepts. That is why every topic in our plan ends with questions and tests, and why Month 5 is all mock exams.

FAQs

CMA Part 2 questions, answered

How long does CMA Part 2 take?

CMA Part 2 takes 5 months with us: 16 weeks (about 4 months) of 48 live classes, then a full review month with 12 mock exams. Plan on about 8 to 10 hours of study each week, including the classes.

How many classes are in CMA Part 2?

There are 48 live classes, held on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from 8 to 10 pm Saudi time. Each class runs 1.5 to 2 hours. Topic-wise and section-wise tests are included within the 16 weeks.

How much does CMA Part 2 cost and can I pay in installments?

CMA Part 2 costs SAR 2,500 (about AED 2,450 or USD 667). You can pay in 3 equal, flexible monthly installments of about SAR 833. AED and USD amounts are approximate and depend on the exchange rate.

What is included in the CMA Part 2 fee?

The fee includes all 48 live classes, 2,000+ MCQs, 30 case-based questions (CBQs), 12 full mock exams, short notes and all topic-wise and section-wise tests. IMA membership, entrance and exam fees are paid separately to IMA.

What topics and weightage does CMA Part 2 cover?

Part 2 has six sections: A. Financial Statement Analysis (20%), B. Corporate Finance (20%), C. Business Decision Analysis (25%), D. Enterprise Risk Management (10%), E. Capital Investment Decisions (10%) and F. Professional Ethics (15%). Our class-by-class plan lists every topic.

What is the CMA Part 2 exam format?

The CMA Part 2 exam is 4 hours long. It has 100 multiple-choice questions in 3 hours (75% of the score), then 2 case-based questions (CBQs) in 1 hour (25%). You need at least 50% on the multiple-choice questions to move on to the CBQs, and a scaled score of 360 out of 500 to pass.

Can I study CMA Part 2 while working full time?

Yes. Classes are live in the evening, 8 to 10 pm Saudi time, three days a week, so they fit around a normal working day. Most working professionals in our batches study about 8 to 10 hours a week.

Can I join Part 2 if I did Part 1 at another institute?

Yes. You are welcome to join our Part 2 batch whether you finished Part 1 with us, with another institute, or through self-study. Book a free orientation and we will walk you through the plan.

Can I take CMA Part 2 before Part 1?

Yes. IMA does not require a fixed order, so you can start with Part 2. Many part-qualified candidates (ACCA, ACA, CPA and similar) do. We will help you decide in your free orientation.

Are the classes in English?

Classes are mainly in English. We use Urdu or Hindi words where they make a concept easier to grasp, and Arabic occasionally.

Are the classes live or recorded?

Classes are live and interactive, and you can ask questions in the session. We do not use pre-recorded lectures. Personal mentor support is available alongside the classes.

How do I pass CMA Part 2?

Learn each topic in class, then practise questions on it the same week. Part 2 is calculation-heavy, so work through ratio, cost of capital, decision and NPV questions often. Take the topic-wise and section-wise tests, study the mistakes, revise, and finish with full mock exams under exam conditions.
