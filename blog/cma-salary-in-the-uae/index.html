Quick answer

In IMA’s 2023 survey of its members in the UAE, the median total pay was $35,398 a year, about AED 10,800 a month. The median base salary was $32,675 a year, AED 10,000 a month. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less. The survey had 153 members, and most were early or mid-career.

Recruiters list pay by job title, and manager roles pay more. Hays says a finance manager in the UAE earns AED 25,000–32,000 a month.

What IMA’s 2023 UAE survey found

IMA is the body that awards the CMA. In August 2023 it asked its members to report their pay. 2,789 members answered worldwide, and 153 of them were usable answers from the UAE. The survey covers IMA members only, so it does not describe the whole UAE market. More than half of the UAE members held the CMA.

Does the CMA raise pay in the UAE?

IMA compares members by certification. Members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median base salary of $32,675 a year, against $29,615 for members with neither. That is about 10% higher.

Median base salary by certification, IMA UAE 2023
GroupBase (USD a year)
Neither CMA nor CPA$29,615
CMA only$32,675
CMA and CPA (11 members)$50,000

The CMA and CPA group has only 11 members, so use it as a guide.

Good to know

The survey shows that the groups earn differently. It does not prove that a certification alone caused the difference. Experience also matters.

What recruiters publish for finance roles in the UAE (AED a month)

Hays and Robert Walters are global recruitment companies. They list pay by job title. These are finance roles that a CMA can hold.

Hays figures are total fixed monthly packages from Q4 2024 data. The guide draws on 1,028 employers and 925 employees. Robert Walters figures are monthly basic salary, without benefits or bonus, based on placements made during 2025 and its predictions for 2026. Hays also lists a finance manager at 25,000 – 32,000 (typical 28,000).

Michael Page is another recruiter. According to What’s On, its UAE Salary Guide 2026 puts accountants in banking and financial services at AED 20,000–27,000 a month. The Finance Story (January 2026) reports general ledger accountants at AED 13,000–18,000 a month in small and medium employers, and AED 15,000–20,000 in multinationals.

How to read these sources

IMA survey
Members’ own pay

153 members, mostly early and mid-career. Yearly pay in US dollars, asked in August 2023.

Hays and Robert Walters
Pay by job title

Roles they fill for employers. Monthly pay in dirhams, from 2024 and 2025 data.

How to use all three
Use as a range

IMA shows pay by experience. The recruiters show pay by role. Together they show what to expect.

A CMA is a certification, not a job title. Employers pay for the role and the experience you bring.

How to estimate your own range

  1. Start with your job title. Find your role in the table above and note the range.
  2. Check your level. Compare your years of experience with the IMA tables above.
  3. Compare like with like. A basic salary, a package with housing and transport, and a total with bonus are three different numbers.
  4. Read the newest guides. Look at the latest Michael Page, Hays and Robert Walters guides before you negotiate.

FAQs

How much does a CMA earn in the UAE?

In IMA’s 2023 survey of its members in the UAE, the median base salary was $32,675 a year (AED 120,000, or AED 10,000 a month). The median total pay was $35,398 a year, about AED 10,800 a month. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less. The survey had 153 members. Pay by job title is higher at manager level, as the Hays and Robert Walters tables show.

What is the CMA salary in Dubai per month in AED?

IMA’s median total pay is about AED 10,800 a month. By job title, Hays’s 2025 guide lists a management accountant at AED 20,000–25,000 a month, and Robert Walters’s 2026 survey lists AED 18,000–30,000. These tables cover the UAE, with no split between Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

Does the CMA increase salary in the UAE?

In IMA’s UAE data, members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median base salary of $32,675 a year. Members with neither certification had $29,615. That is about 10% higher. Members with both certifications had $50,000, but that group was only 11 people. The survey shows the groups earn differently. It does not prove that a certification caused the difference.

What is the starting salary of a CMA in Dubai?

IMA’s survey shows a median total pay of $23,917 a year (about AED 7,300 a month) for entry-level and lower management members. Members with 0–9 years of experience had a median base of $16,338 a year (about AED 5,000 a month). Hays’s 2025 guide lists an accountant at AED 10,000–15,000 a month. Robert Walters’s 2026 survey lists a general ledger accountant at AED 15,000–20,000.

What is the salary of a finance manager in Dubai?

Hays’s GCC Salary Guide 2025 lists a finance manager in the UAE at AED 25,000–32,000 a month, with a typical figure of AED 28,000. It is a fixed monthly package, from Q4 2024 data. Robert Walters’s 2026 survey lists a regional finance manager at AED 25,000–45,000 a month in basic salary.

What is a management accountant’s salary in the UAE?

Hays’s 2025 guide lists AED 20,000–25,000 a month, with a typical figure of AED 22,500. Robert Walters’s 2026 survey lists AED 18,000–30,000 a month in basic salary, with an average of AED 24,000. The CMA is a common certification for this kind of role.

Is CMA salary higher in the UAE or Saudi Arabia?

The two are close. IMA’s 2023 median total pay is $35,398 in the UAE and $38,667 in Saudi Arabia (KSA). In Hays’s guides for the same period, a finance manager’s typical pay is about US$7,600 a month in the UAE and about US$10,700 in Saudi Arabia. See CMA salary in Saudi Arabia (KSA).

Is the CMA worth it in the UAE?

UAE members who answered IMA’s survey were positive. Of them, 86% would recommend the CMA, 86% said it gave them confidence, 77% said it improved their career opportunities and 51% said it brought a higher salary. Our guide to the US CMA in the UAE covers recognition, employers and how to study.
