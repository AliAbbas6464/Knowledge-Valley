Quick answer

In IMA’s 2023 Global Salary Survey, median total pay for members ranged from $6,007 a year in Egypt to $135,000 in the USA. Canada was $81,251, Saudi Arabia (KSA) $38,667, the UAE $35,398 and Qatar $31,061. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less.

The table covers IMA members, with or without the CMA. We also checked each country against independent sources, such as government data and recruiter guides.

APQC surveyed all IMA members in August 2023 for IMA. There were 2,789 responses from 69 countries, and 2,572 were usable. Results are in 2023 US dollars. They cover IMA members only.

Base salary is regular pay. Total pay adds bonuses and other pay. Saudi Arabia (KSA) and the UAE counts come from IMA’s country reports. A higher number in US dollars does not always mean a better standard of living. Prices differ from country to country.

Recruiters list pay by job title. Hays’s 2025 guides (Q4 2024 data) put a finance manager at a typical SAR 40,000 a month in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and AED 28,000 in the UAE, as total fixed packages. Qatar Living’s guide (24 July 2026) gives typical ranges of QAR 9,000–16,000 a month for an accountant with two to five years’ experience and QAR 28,000–45,000 for a finance manager.

What the CMA adds worldwide

IMA compares members by certification. The median base salary was $41,272 a year for members with the CMA only, and $36,417 for members with neither. That is about 13% higher. IMA also reports that the CMA helps most early in a career: the median salary gain linked to the CMA was 32% for Gen Z members.

Good to know

The survey shows that the groups earn differently. It does not prove that the CMA alone caused the difference. Experience and country also matter.

How to use these numbers

  1. Pick your country. Use the IMA median for a general picture and a recruiter guide for your job title.
  2. Check the unit. Note whether a figure is base salary or total pay, a month or a year, and which currency.
  3. Check how many people answered. A country with about 25 members is only a guide.
  4. Weigh more than pay. In IMA’s UAE survey, 86% of members said the CMA gave them confidence and 51% said it brought a higher salary, so decide using the roles you want.

FAQs

Which country pays the highest CMA salary?

In IMA’s 2023 survey, the USA had the highest median total pay: $135,000 a year. The Netherlands ($108,495) and Canada ($81,251) came next. These are medians for IMA members, with or without the CMA. The Netherlands and Canada had only about 50 members each.

What is the average CMA salary worldwide?

Across all members in IMA’s 2023 survey, in 69 countries, the median base salary was $45,873 a year and the median total pay was $54,398. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less. Pay differs a lot by country, so check the table above.

How much does a CMA earn in the USA?

US IMA members reported a median base salary of $122,000 and a median total pay of $135,000 a year in 2023. For all US accountants and auditors, the Bureau of Labor Statistics median wage was $83,680 in May 2025. The first covers IMA members. The second covers the whole occupation.

How much does a CMA earn in Canada?

Canadian IMA members reported a median base salary of $74,351 and a median total pay of $81,251 a year in US dollars in 2023. Job Bank Canada, a government source, shows a median wage of $40.36 an hour for financial auditors and accountants, updated 19 November 2025.

How much does a CMA earn in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar?

IMA’s 2023 median total pay is about AED 10,800 a month in the UAE, SAR 12,100 in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and QAR 9,400 in Qatar. We converted these from US dollars at the fixed rates. Qatar had only about 25 members. Our UAE and Saudi Arabia (KSA) guides add recruiter pay by job title.

Does a CMA earn more than someone without one?

In IMA’s 2023 survey, the median base salary was $41,272 a year for members with the CMA only and $36,417 for members with neither. That is about 13% higher. The survey shows the groups earn differently. It does not prove that the CMA alone caused the difference.

Which countries have the most members in IMA’s salary survey?

The most members came from the USA (about 800) and China (about 750). Next came the UAE (153), Egypt (about 100), Saudi Arabia (KSA) (96) and the Philippines (about 80). Countries with about 50 members or fewer, such as Canada and Qatar, give only a guide.

How should I compare CMA salaries between countries?

Check four things: the currency, whether it is base salary or total pay, whether it is a month or a year, and how many people answered. Then compare each country with the cost of living there.
