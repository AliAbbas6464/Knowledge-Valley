Quick answer

Yes, the US CMA (Certified Management Accountant, also called CMA USA) is recognised (recognized) in the UAE (United Arab Emirates) as an international professional certification. In August 2025 the UAE Ministry of Finance signed an MoU with IMA to launch the CMA and FMAA for federal accountants. UAE employers list the CMA in finance job requirements, usually as preferred, and IMA has chapters in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

It is a certification, not a statutory licence, and it does not teach UAE corporate tax law.

Is the CMA backed by the UAE government? The Ministry of Finance MoU

On 6 August 2025 the UAE Ministry of Finance announced a strategic memorandum of understanding with IMA, signed at the Ministry’s headquarters in Abu Dhabi.

This shows that a federal ministry chose the CMA for its own staff. It does not set rules for private employers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah, where each employer decides.

Does the CMA cover UAE corporate tax? What it builds and what it does not

The UAE’s federal corporate tax applies to financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023. The rate is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that, according to the Ministry of Finance. Finance teams now plan budgets, costs and profit with a tax charge in view. Here is what the CMA does and does not cover.

What the CMA builds
  • Financial planning, performance and analytics (Part 1)
  • Strategic financial management (Part 2)
  • Budgeting, costing and decision-making
What it does not teach
  • UAE corporate tax law
  • Federal Tax Authority registration and filing
  • For these, use the FTA’s guidance or a tax adviser

Many UAE roles need both tax knowledge and management accounting, so the two skill sets support each other.

CMA UAE: the US CMA or the Capital Market Authority?

If you search “CMA UAE”, you may see results about a regulator, not the certification.

CMA (US)
  • Certified Management Accountant, a certification from IMA
  • For finance professionals, in two exam parts
Capital Market Authority
  • The UAE’s federal securities regulator
  • The successor to the Securities and Commodities Authority, according to the CFA Institute

Add “IMA”, “US” or “Certified Management Accountant” to your search to find the right one.

FAQs

Is the CMA USA certificate valid in Dubai and the UAE?

The certificate is issued by IMA and is accepted as an international credential by the UAE employers who list it in their requirements. The Ministry of Finance has also signed an MoU with IMA to launch the CMA for federal accountants. It is a certification, not a statutory licence, and each employer decides how much weight to give it.

Is the US CMA recognised in the UAE?

Yes, as an international professional certification. In August 2025 the UAE Ministry of Finance signed an MoU with IMA to launch the CMA and FMAA certifications for the Ministry and federal government entities. It is a certification, not a statutory licence.

What did the UAE Ministry of Finance agree with IMA?

Under the MoU, signed in Abu Dhabi, the Ministry and IMA will offer training leading to the CMA and FMAA for employees of the Ministry and federal entities, with a focus on developing national talent. They also plan joint events such as conferences, seminars and workshops.

Do UAE employers ask for the CMA?

Some do. In UAE job listings on Jooble, we saw the CMA listed as preferred, highly preferred or an advantage for finance manager, project cost accountant and accounting roles in Dubai, usually alongside CPA, ACCA or CA. Requirements vary, so read the specific job description.

Does the CMA teach UAE corporate tax?

No. The CMA is a global certification from IMA, so it does not teach UAE corporate tax law. It builds planning, budgeting, costing and decision-making skills. For UAE corporate tax rules, use the Federal Tax Authority’s guidance or a tax adviser.

What is the UAE corporate tax rate?

According to the UAE Ministry of Finance, corporate tax applies to financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023, at 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above that. Some free zone and sector rules differ, so check the Federal Tax Authority for your case.

What is the difference between the CMA (US) and the Capital Market Authority?

The CMA (US) is the Certified Management Accountant certification from IMA. In the UAE, the Capital Market Authority (also abbreviated CMA) is the federal securities regulator and the successor to the Securities and Commodities Authority, according to the CFA Institute. Add “IMA” or “US” to your search to find the certification.

Are there IMA chapters in the UAE?

Yes. The IMA Dubai Chapter was founded in 2007 for members in Dubai and the Northern Emirates, and the Abu Dhabi Chapter was chartered in 2014. IMA Middle East also lists student chapters at the American University of Sharjah and the University of Sharjah.

Which UAE universities have IMA endorsement?

Al Ain University’s accounting programme earned IMA endorsement in August 2021, and IMA described it as the 100th endorsed school worldwide. Endorsement means IMA reviewed the curriculum for coverage of CMA exam content.
