Quick answer

Yes, the US CMA (Certified Management Accountant, also called CMA USA) is recognised (recognized) in Qatar as an international professional certification. Qatar University’s accounting programme is IMA-endorsed, Qatar University signed an MoU with IMA in February 2021, and IMA has a Doha Chapter. The CMA exam is administered through Prometric centres.

It is a certification, not a licence. Qatari employers decide how much weight to give it, so check your target role.

Where and when do you take the CMA exam in Qatar?

Where
Prometric

FAQs

Is the CMA USA certificate valid in Doha and Qatar?

The certificate is issued by IMA and is accepted as an international credential. Qatar University’s accounting programme is IMA-endorsed, and IMA has a Doha Chapter. It is a certification, not a licence, and each employer decides how much weight to give it.

Is the US CMA recognised in Qatar?

Yes, as an international professional certification. IMA says it has offered the CMA in Qatar for almost 50 years, Qatar University’s accounting programme is IMA-endorsed, and Qatar University signed an MoU with IMA in February 2021. It is a certification, not a licence.

What is IMA endorsement at Qatar University?

IMA endorses accounting programmes whose curriculum covers the CMA exam content. Qatar University says a programme must document that at least 75% of the tested CMA topics are covered. QU described itself as one of 43 institutions worldwide with the endorsement at that time, and the fourth in the Middle East.

What did IMA and Qatar University agree in 2021?

According to Gulf Times (23 February 2021), the MoU lets Qatar University offer CMA materials to students, and IMA will provide CMA scholarships to the top ten distinguished QU students from all specialisations. They also plan to support faculty research.

Where do I take the CMA exam if I live in Qatar?

IMA Middle East says CMA exams in Qatar are administered through the global network of Prometric centres. Use Prometric’s IMA locator to check options, and confirm IMA’s current exam windows before you book.

Is there an IMA chapter in Qatar?

Yes. IMA has a Doha Chapter, which describes itself as the official IMA chapter for Doha.

Does the CMA count for Qatari employers?

It depends on the employer and the role. The CMA is widely used as an international finance credential, and IMA says it has partnered with Qatar institutions for it. Read the job description and ask your employer whether they value it for promotion or training budgets.
