Quick answer

In IMA’s 2023 survey of its members in Saudi Arabia (KSA), the median total pay was $38,667 a year, about SAR 12,100 a month. The median base salary was $31,000 a year, about SAR 9,700 a month. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less. The survey had 96 members.

Members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median total pay 31% higher than members with neither. Recruiters list pay by job title instead. Hays says a finance manager in Saudi Arabia earns SAR 25,000–45,000 a month.

What IMA’s 2023 KSA survey found

IMA is the body that awards the CMA. In August 2023 it asked its members to report their pay. 2,789 members answered worldwide, and 96 of them were usable answers from Saudi Arabia (KSA). The survey covers IMA members only, so it does not describe the whole Saudi market.

Does the CMA raise pay in KSA?

IMA compares members by certification. Members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median total pay of $40,960 a year, against $31,167 for members with neither. That is 31% higher. Their median base salary was also 31% higher.

Median pay by certification, IMA KSA 2023
GroupMembers (about)Base (USD a year)Total pay (USD a year)
Neither CMA nor CPA46$24,828$31,167
CMA only41$32,533$40,960

The CMA and CPA group is only a handful of people, so use it as a guide.

Good to know

The survey shows that the groups earn differently. It does not prove that the CMA alone caused the difference. Experience also matters.

Another 90% would recommend the CMA to a friend or colleague.

How to read these two sources

IMA survey
Members’ own pay

96 members, from early career to senior. Yearly pay in US dollars, asked in August 2023.

Hays guide
Pay by job title

Roles Hays fills for employers. A fixed monthly package in riyals, from Q4 2024.

How to use both
Use as a range

IMA shows pay by experience. Hays shows pay by role. Together they show what to expect.

A CMA is a certification, not a job title. Employers pay for the role and the experience you bring.

How to estimate your own range

  1. Start with your job title. Find your role in the Hays table and note the range.
  2. Check your level. Compare your years of experience with the IMA tables above.
  3. Compare like with like. Check if an offer is a base salary or a package with housing and transport, and if a bonus is extra.
  4. Read the newest guides. Look at the 2026 Hays Saudi guide before you negotiate.

FAQs

How much does a CMA earn in Saudi Arabia?

In IMA’s 2023 survey of its members in Saudi Arabia (KSA), the median base salary was $31,000 a year, about SAR 9,700 a month. The median total pay was $38,667 a year, about SAR 12,100 a month. “Median” means the middle figure: half of the members earn more and half earn less. The survey had 96 members.

What is the CMA salary in Saudi Arabia per month?

The median total pay in IMA’s survey is about SAR 12,100 a month. By job title, Hays’s 2025 Saudi guide lists a senior accountant at SAR 20,000–28,000 a month and a finance manager at SAR 25,000–45,000 a month.

Does the CMA increase salary in Saudi Arabia?

In IMA’s survey, members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median total pay of $40,960 a year. Members with neither certification had $31,167. That is 31% higher. The survey shows the two groups earn differently. It does not prove that the CMA alone caused the difference.

What is the starting salary of a CMA in Saudi Arabia?

IMA’s survey shows a median total pay of $16,000 a year (about SAR 5,000 a month) for entry-level and lower management members. For members with 0–9 years of experience it is $25,600 a year (about SAR 8,000 a month). Hays’s 2025 guide lists an accountant at SAR 10,000–20,000 a month.

What is the salary of a finance manager in Saudi Arabia?

Hays’s Saudi Arabia Salary Guide 2025 lists a finance manager at SAR 25,000–45,000 a month, with a typical figure of SAR 40,000. This is a fixed monthly package, from Hays data for the fourth quarter of 2024. It covers Saudi Arabia as a whole, not Riyadh alone.

Is CMA salary higher in Saudi Arabia or the UAE?

The two are close. IMA’s 2023 median total pay is $38,667 in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and $35,398 in the UAE. In Hays’s guides for the same period, a finance manager’s typical pay is SAR 40,000 a month in Saudi Arabia (about US$10,700) and AED 28,000 in the UAE (about US$7,600). See CMA salary in the UAE.

Is the CMA worth it in Saudi Arabia?

Saudi members who answered IMA’s survey were positive. Of them, 90% would recommend the CMA, 92% said it gave them confidence at work, and 81% said it improved their career opportunities. Compare it with the roles you want. Our guide to the CMA in Saudi Arabia (KSA) explains HRDF funding and how employers ask for the CMA.

Does a CMA plus CPA earn more in Saudi Arabia?

In IMA’s data, members with both certifications had a median total pay of $72,000 a year. CMA only was $40,960 and neither was $31,167. The both-certifications group had only about 7 of the 96 members, so treat it as a guide, not a rule.
