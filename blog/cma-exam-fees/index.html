Quick answer

For a professional member, the IMA fees for the US CMA total $1,685 in the first year. That is about AED 6,188 or SAR 6,319. This covers IMA membership ($295), the one-time entrance fee ($300) and the exam fee for both parts ($545 each).

Student members pay $1,088 in total, about AED 3,996 or SAR 4,080. With an IMA promotion of 50% off the membership and entrance fee, a professional pays about $1,388 (AED 5,096 or SAR 5,203). Study materials and coaching are extra.

Check before you pay

IMA’s past offers said the discount was for the membership and entrance fee, for new members only. It did not apply to membership renewals. Exam discounts, when offered, came as a separate code by email after you bought the bundle and applied to one testing window. Read the terms of the current offer.

  1. Watch IMA Middle East. Offers and codes appear on its website and social pages, usually for a few weeks.
  2. Buy the membership and entrance bundle first. Enter the code at checkout.
  3. Wait for the exam code if one is offered. IMA emails it after the bundle is bought. Use it for the exam window named in the offer.
  4. Time your start. The three-year entrance window begins when you pay, so buy when you are ready to study.

Other costs to plan for

The IMA total is only part of the cost. Plan for these as well:

Retaking a part
$545 each time

If you do not pass, you pay the exam fee again for that part.

Rescheduling
$50 at Prometric

Changing or cancelling an appointment costs a fee. Book with care.

Yearly renewal
Membership + $30

CMAs pay the maintenance fee and renew membership every year.

Study materials and coaching are separate from IMA’s fees. Ask what is included before you pay: classes, question practice, mock exams and support.

How to keep the cost down

  1. Join as the right member type. If you are a student or an academic, use the lower fees.
  2. Pay the entrance fee when you are ready. The three-year window starts when you pay it.
  3. Book an exam date you can keep. A change costs $50 and wastes time.
  4. Aim to pass the first time. A retake costs the full exam fee again, about AED 2,002 or SAR 2,044.

FAQs

How much does the CMA exam cost in total?

For a professional member, the IMA fees add up to $1,685 for the first year: $295 membership, $300 entrance fee and $545 for each of the two exam parts. That is about AED 6,188 or SAR 6,319. Student members pay $1,088 (about AED 3,996 or SAR 4,080). This total does not include study materials or coaching.

How much is the CMA exam fee in AED?

One exam part costs $545 for a professional member, about AED 2,002. Both parts cost $1,090, about AED 4,003. The full IMA cost in the first year is about AED 6,188. We convert at the fixed rate of AED 3.6725 per US dollar.

How much is the CMA exam fee in SAR (Saudi riyals)?

One exam part costs $545 for a professional member, about SAR 2,044. Both parts cost $1,090, about SAR 4,088. The full IMA cost in the first year is about SAR 6,319. We convert at the fixed rate of SAR 3.75 per US dollar.

What is the CMA entrance fee?

The entrance fee is a one-time payment to enter the CMA programme. It is $300 for professional members and $225 for student and academic members. It is valid for three years, and you must pass both parts in that time. If you do not, a part you passed expires and you pay the entrance fee again.

Do I pay the CMA exam fee in dollars?

Yes. IMA lists every fee in US dollars. Your bank or card converts the amount to dirhams or riyals, so the final figure can differ a little because of bank charges.

What if I fail a CMA part?

You pay the exam fee again to retake that part: $545 for a professional member. IMA does not list a separate retake fee. Because of this, a failed attempt costs about AED 2,002 or SAR 2,044, which is why a good study plan saves money.

What is the CMA rescheduling fee?

Prometric, the test centre company, charges a $50 fee (about AED 184 or SAR 188) to reschedule or cancel an appointment, when you do it within the time limit. Check the Prometric page for the exact rules before you book.

Is there a yearly fee after I become a CMA?

Yes. To keep the certification, CMAs renew their IMA membership and pay the CMA Annual Maintenance Fee of $30 every year. That is about AED 110 or SAR 113 for the maintenance fee, plus the membership fee.

How much does it cost to become a CMA in Dubai or Saudi Arabia in total?

A professional member pays $1,685 in IMA fees in the first year, about AED 6,188 or SAR 6,319. Add live classes at SAR 2,500 per part (about AED 2,448) and the full budget is about AED 11,085 or SAR 11,319, if you pass both parts first time. The IMA fees are the same in every country because IMA bills in US dollars.

Are the CMA exam fees the same in the UAE and Saudi Arabia?

Yes. IMA charges the same US dollar fees worldwide. Only the amount in dirhams or riyals changes with the exchange rate. Both currencies are fixed to the dollar, so the figures in this guide stay steady.

Can I pay for CMA classes in installments?

At Knowledge Valley you can pay for each part in three equal installments, about SAR 833 a month for SAR 2,500. IMA’s own fees are paid to IMA when you register, in US dollars.

Does IMA give a discount on the CMA fees?

Yes, often. IMA Middle East has run offers of 40% or 50% off the IMA membership and the CMA entrance fee, and at times 25% off the exam fees. With 50% off the membership and entrance fee, a professional member pays about $1,388 in the first year, about AED 5,096 or SAR 5,203. Offers are for a limited time, so check IMA’s website for the current code.

How much is the CMA with a 40% discount?

With 40% off the membership and entrance fee, a professional member pays about $1,447 in the first year, about AED 5,314 or SAR 5,426. A student member pays about $978, about AED 3,593 or SAR 3,669.

Are student fees cheaper?

Yes. Student members pay $49 for membership, $225 for the entrance fee and $407 per part. Academic members pay $160 for membership. Check IMA’s student membership page to see if you qualify.
