Is the US CMA Recognised in Saudi Arabia (KSA)? HRDF Funding, Jobs and How to Study While You Work

Quick answer

Yes, the US CMA (Certified Management Accountant, also called CMA USA) is recognized in Saudi Arabia (KSA) as an international professional certification, and it is supported in three ways: HRDF accredited it so Saudi nationals can seek reimbursement, universities such as KFUPM and Prince Sultan University work with IMA, and Saudi employers list it in finance job requirements, usually as preferred.

It does not replace SOCPA, which is the route for the Saudi CPA. If you are an expatriate working in Saudi Arabia, HRDF funding does not apply to you.

Does HRDF pay for the CMA? Who can claim in Saudi Arabia

HRDF’s funding depends on who you are. Find the card that describes you.

A Saudi national, self-funded
May be eligible

The CMA must be on HRDF’s current approved list. Exam costs are reimbursed once, and only if you pass.

A Saudi national, employer pays
Not under this programme

HRDF says the employer must not have paid for the certificate.

An expatriate in Saudi Arabia
No

The programme is for Saudi nationals only. Ask your employer about a training budget.

Membership fees are not covered. Read HRDF’s page for the current conditions before you pay for anything.

Do Saudi employers ask for the CMA? What job posts say

“SOCPA required; CPA or CMA is a plus”SOCPA comes first for that role, and the CMA adds to it.
“SOCPA, CMA, ACCA, CPA etc. is an advantage”The employer values a mix of certifications.

CMA or SOCPA: which one do you need in Saudi Arabia?

SOCPA
  • The mandatory body for CPAs in Saudi Arabia, according to IFAC
  • Candidates pass SOCPA’s own professional examinations
  • Some Saudi job listings require registration
CMA (US)
  • IMA’s certification in management accounting and financial management
  • Planning, budgeting, costing and decision-making
  • Not a licence to audit

The two can sit side by side on one CV.

CMA Saudi Arabia: the US CMA or the Capital Market Authority?

If you search “CMA Saudi Arabia”, you may see results about a regulator, not the certification.

CMA (US)
  • Certified Management Accountant, a certification from IMA
  • For finance professionals, in two exam parts
Capital Market Authority
  • The government regulator of the Saudi capital market
  • Sets qualification examinations for people in the securities industry

Add “IMA”, “US” or “Certified Management Accountant” to your search to find the right one.

How to study the CMA in Saudi Arabia while working (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar)

  1. Collect three job descriptions. Choose roles you want in Riyadh, Jeddah or Dammam and note how each one writes CMA, SOCPA and CPA.
  2. Check funding. If you are a Saudi national, check HRDF’s list and conditions first. If you are an expatriate, ask your employer about a training budget.
  3. Choose evening live classes. After-work classes let you keep your job while you study each part.
  4. Practise the exam format early. Case-based questions (CBQs) carry real weight, so start with how to answer CBQs and then try the worked example.
  5. Book your exam window. Check IMA’s current windows and the Prometric options available to you.

FAQs

Is the US CMA recognised in Saudi Arabia (KSA)?

Yes, as an international professional certification. IMA announced that Saudi Arabia’s Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF) accredited the CMA, so Saudi national candidates can seek reimbursement of training and exam costs. Saudi employers also list the CMA in finance job requirements, usually as preferred. It is not a licence to sign audits: that sits with SOCPA.

Is the CMA USA certificate valid in KSA?

The certificate is issued by IMA and is accepted as an international credential by the Saudi employers who list it in their requirements. It is a certification, not a licence to practise, and each employer decides how much weight to give it, so check the job description.

Is the US CMA accepted by employers in Saudi Arabia?

Many Saudi employers list it. In Saudi job listings on Jooble, we saw the CMA written as preferred, highly preferred or a plus, usually alongside CPA, ACCA, CA or SOCPA, and in at least one listing as a requirement (“CMA qualification or equivalent”). Wording differs by employer and role, so read the specific job description.

Does HRDF pay for the CMA in Saudi Arabia?

It can, for eligible Saudi nationals. IMA announced that HRDF accredited the CMA so Saudi national candidates who complete the programme can seek reimbursement of training costs and exam fees. HRDF’s conditions say the certificate must be on its approved list, your employer must not have paid, exam costs are reimbursed once and only if you pass, and membership fees are not covered.

I am an expatriate in Saudi Arabia. Can I get HRDF support?

No. HRDF’s Professional Certification Support program is for Saudi nationals only. Expatriates normally pay themselves or ask their employer about a training budget.

Should I do SOCPA or the CMA first?

It depends on the job. SOCPA is the mandatory body for CPAs in Saudi Arabia, and some listings require SOCPA registration. The CMA is IMA’s certification for management accounting, so it fits roles in planning, costing and performance. Check the roles you want.

Are there IMA chapters in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. The IMA Riyadh Chapter was formed in 2013 to promote finance and management accounting professions in Saudi Arabia, and it supports the Prince Sultan University student chapter. IMA Middle East also lists a KFUPM student chapter in the Eastern Province.

What is the difference between the CMA (US) and the Capital Market Authority?

The CMA (US) is the Certified Management Accountant certification from IMA. In Saudi Arabia, the Capital Market Authority (also abbreviated CMA) is the government regulator of the capital market, and it sets qualification examinations for people registered in the securities industry. Add “IMA” or “US” to your search to find the certification.

Which Saudi universities work with IMA?

IMA announced a memorandum of understanding with King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) in January 2021 to introduce the CMA programme to its students, and IMA endorsed the accounting programme at Prince Sultan University in Riyadh in 2016. IMA also signed an MoU with The Financial Academy in Saudi Arabia in March 2021.

Can I study the CMA in Riyadh, Jeddah or Dammam while working full time?

Yes. Knowledge Valley teaches CMA Part 1 and Part 2 in live evening classes online, so you can attend from Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam or Khobar after work. Each part includes 30 case-based questions (CBQs) and 12 full mock exams.

Where do I take the CMA exam if I live in Saudi Arabia?

IMA delivers the CMA exam through Prometric. Use the locator on Prometric’s IMA page to find the centre or the options available to you, and check IMA’s current exam windows before booking.
