Quick answer

Yes, you can add the US CMA after ACCA, and you do not have to finish ACCA first. There is no education requirement to sit the CMA exams. ACCA members also get IMA’s bachelor’s degree waiver. The CMA has two exams, and you have three years to pass them.

ACCA is a broad accountancy qualification. The CMA is specialised in management accounting. They work well together, so most finance professionals add one to the other and do not drop one for the other.

This guide is for ACCA students and members in the UAE, Saudi Arabia (KSA) and the wider Gulf. It is fair to ACCA, which is a respected qualification. It shows how the CMA can sit next to it, and it explains why a part-qualified ACCA student is well placed to start the CMA now. Most of our students are part-qualified ACCA candidates, and they are the reason we wrote it.

Who should add the CMA after ACCA?

Add the CMA when you want to grow in the management side of finance. These are the roles it points to:

  • FP&A analyst or manager, budgeting and forecasting.
  • Cost accountant and management accountant.
  • Commercial finance and finance business partner.
  • Performance reporting and finance manager roles in a company.

Stay with ACCA alone if your plan is audit, tax or a practice route. ACCA is built for these. The CMA does not replace it there.

Good to know

In our experience, many finance candidates in the Gulf study ACCA, and fewer add a US credential. A CMA next to ACCA can help your CV stand out for management finance roles. It is not a promise of a job or a pay rise.

Part-qualified in ACCA? Start the CMA now

If you have passed some ACCA papers but not all of them, this is good news. You do not need to wait for the last exam.

  1. Sit the exams first, show your education later. IMA has no education requirement to sit the CMA exams. You show a bachelor’s degree or an approved professional certification within seven years of passing.
  2. Finishing ACCA helps you later. When you become an ACCA member, IMA waives its bachelor’s degree requirement for you. Your ACCA effort pays off twice.
  3. You already know many of the topics. ACCA papers such as Performance Management and Financial Management cover costing, budgeting and investment appraisal. The CMA studies the same ground in more depth, plus planning, performance, risk and ethics.
  4. You have time on your side. You have three years to pass both parts after paying the entrance fee, and seven years to meet all the CMA requirements.
  5. You study the way you already do. Our live CMA classes run in the evening on Saudi time. You can plan around your ACCA sittings and your job.

The result is a second credential, a US one, while you are still on your ACCA path. It also gives you a clear goal to work towards between ACCA sittings.

A study plan for an ACCA student

  1. Pick the month. Choose one of IMA’s three testing windows a year and work back from the date.
  2. Start with Part 1. It covers external financial reporting decisions, planning and budgeting, performance management, cost management and internal controls, which link to what you know from ACCA.
  3. Practise with case questions. Each part at Knowledge Valley includes 30 case-based questions (CBQs) and 12 full mock exams. Read how to answer CMA case-based questions for the method.
  4. Take Part 2 next. It covers financial statement analysis, corporate finance, decision analysis, risk, investment decisions and ethics.
  5. Plan the cost. Check the CMA fees in USD, AED and SAR and any IMA discount code before you pay.

FAQs

Is CMA better than ACCA in the Gulf?

Neither is better for everyone. ACCA is a broad accountancy qualification that covers reporting, audit, tax and management. The US CMA focuses on management accounting: planning, budgeting, costing, performance and corporate finance. Choose by the role you want. If you want FP&A, cost, commercial finance or finance business partner roles, the CMA fits closely. Many finance professionals in the Gulf hold both.

What is the difference between ACCA and CMA?

ACCA is a broad accountancy qualification with 13 exams and 36 months of experience. The US CMA is a management accounting certification with two exam parts and two years of experience. ACCA covers reporting, audit, tax and management. The CMA goes deep into planning, performance, cost and corporate finance.

Can I do the CMA after ACCA?

Yes. ACCA itself says members can earn the CMA by passing the two-part CMA exam and completing two years of experience in a management accounting role. IMA also waives the bachelor’s degree requirement for ACCA members.

Can I start the CMA before I finish ACCA?

Yes. IMA has no education requirement to sit the CMA exams. You show your education (a bachelor’s degree or an approved professional certification such as ACCA membership) within seven years of passing, and you have seven years in total to meet all the CMA requirements. This is why part-qualified ACCA students can begin now.

Does ACCA give me exemptions in the CMA exam?

No exam exemptions are published. ACCA’s own page says its members earn the CMA after passing the two-part CMA exam. What IMA gives ACCA members is the bachelor’s degree waiver. The topics you studied in ACCA will still help you, especially in performance management and financial management.

Should I finish ACCA first or start the CMA now?

It depends on where you are. If you are close to your last ACCA exams, finish them first. If you have several papers left, or a long wait between sittings, the CMA can run next to ACCA, one part at a time. Do not sit an ACCA paper and a CMA part in the same weeks. See the stage cards above.

How many exams is the CMA compared with ACCA?

The CMA has two exam parts. ACCA has 13 exams across three levels, plus an Ethics and Professional Skills module and 36 months of practical experience. The CMA needs two continuous years of experience in management accounting or financial management.

Which pays more in the Gulf, ACCA or CMA?

We could not find a published figure that compares the two directly, so we do not give one. Pay depends on your role, your experience and your employer. IMA’s 2023 survey shows that Saudi members with the CMA (and no CPA) had a median total pay 31% higher than members with neither certification. See CMA salary in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and CMA salary in the UAE.

How much does the CMA cost for an ACCA student?

IMA’s fees for a professional member are $1,685 in the first year (about AED 6,188 or SAR 6,319). Student members pay $1,088. IMA runs promotions with 40% or 50% off the membership and entrance fee. Read CMA exam fees in USD, AED and SAR.
