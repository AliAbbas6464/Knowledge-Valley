Frequently asked questions

Is the US CMA recognised in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and the UAE?
Our country guides explain how employers, regulators and funding schemes treat the CMA. Start with the CMA in Saudi Arabia (KSA) and the CMA in the UAE. Each guide names its sources.
How much does the CMA cost in AED and SAR?
For a professional member, IMA’s fees total $1,685 in the first year, about AED 6,188 or SAR 6,319. This covers membership, the entrance fee and both exam parts. IMA often runs 40% and 50% discount offers on the membership and entrance fee. See CMA exam fees in USD, AED and SAR.
What is the CMA salary in Saudi Arabia (KSA)?
In IMA’s 2023 survey of 96 members in Saudi Arabia (KSA), the median total pay was $38,667 a year, about SAR 12,100 a month. Read CMA salary in Saudi Arabia (KSA) for pay by level and by job title.
What is the CMA salary in the UAE?
In IMA’s 2023 survey of 153 members in the UAE, the median total pay was $35,398 a year, about AED 10,800 a month. Read CMA salary in the UAE for the full tables, and CMA salary by country to compare.
Can I do the CMA after ACCA?
Yes. There is no education requirement to sit the CMA exams, and IMA waives its bachelor’s degree requirement for ACCA members. Part-qualified ACCA students can start now. See CMA after ACCA.
What are CMA case-based questions (CBQs)?
Case-based questions (CBQs) replaced the essay section of the CMA exam. You read a business case and answer several linked questions. See what changed and how to answer them.
Are live or recorded CMA classes better for working professionals?
For most working professionals, live classes work better because a fixed time builds the habit and questions are answered in the moment. Read live vs recorded CMA classes for the reasons and the research.
